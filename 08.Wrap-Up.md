## Troubleshooting

- **`ModuleNotFoundError: fairlearn`** — run `pip install fairlearn`.
- **`ThresholdOptimizer` errors about a fitted estimator** — pass the already-fitted `clf` and set `prefit=True` and `predict_method="predict_proba"`, as in Section 5.
- **Your numbers differ slightly from the ones quoted** — small differences across scikit-learn or fairlearn versions are normal; the *direction* of every result (parity passes, the two gaps trade off, zero leakage) is what matters and is stable.
- **A metric is `nan` for a group** — that group had no predicted positives or no actual positives in a cell; check the per-group counts from Section 1.
