# Submitting This Lab (graded)

**Deadline: 23:59 on the Wednesday after the lab.** Late work counts but is recorded as late.

## 1. Finish the sheet

Every question in `SUBMISSION.md` should have an answer, including the predictions you got wrong. Do not
change a prediction afterwards: say whether it was right in the question that follows it. Add your name,
student number and project dataset at the top.

## 2. Build your zip

In the lab folder:

```bash
python make_submission.py
```

It asks for your student number the first time, checks that no `TODO` is left, runs a quick environment
check, and writes one zip named after you (for example `submission-week08-00123456.zip`) with your notebook,
`SUBMISSION.md` and figures. The credit data and the helper files are left out.

## 3. Upload it

Upload that one zip to the Week 8 OneDrive folder on the course site.

## How it is marked

Each graded lab is marked out of 100 on four criteria: completeness 25%, correctness and reproducibility 30%,
application to your own project data 20%, and reasoning 25%. The seven graded labs are averaged, and together
count 20% of the module. Reference answers are published after the deadline.
