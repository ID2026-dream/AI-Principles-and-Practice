# AI Principles and Practice

data · supervised · ethics · safety · representations · neural networks · mathematics · transfer · transformers · diffusion

Fundamentals first, frontier earned. A twelve-week postgraduate module that builds the
foundations first — data, evaluation, ethics, safety, representations — and
then earns its way to neural networks, transformers and diffusion. Every week
in the second half cashes in something taught in the first.

Assessed by a continuous lab portfolio, a Week 6 quiz, an applied assignment,
and a Week 12 end-to-end project on an unseen dataset defended in person.
