# Assessment

[[toc]]

| Component | Weight | LOs | Timing |
|---|---|---|---|
| Lab portfolio (continuous project notebooks) | 25% | LO1 to LO3 | Continuous |
| Quiz | 15% | LO1, LO2 | Week 6 |
| Applied assignment: modelling with an ethics, fairness and explainability audit | 20% | LO2, LO3, LO5 | Around Week 7 |
| Final project and live defence: end-to-end AI on an unseen dataset | 40% | LO3, LO4, LO5 | Week 12 |

## Lab portfolio — 25%, continuous

The notebooks you produce in the weekly labs, carried forward on a single
project dataset of your own choosing. It is continuous because the point is the
trail: Week 1's data dictionary is what Week 3 audits, and Week 7's training
curves are what Week 8 explains.

## Quiz — 15%, Week 6

In-class, covering Weeks 1 to 5: data analysis; supervised learning; ethics,
bias and fairness; AI safety, governance and explainability; and unsupervised
learning.

## Applied assignment — 20%, around Week 7

Modelling with an ethics, fairness and explainability audit attached. This is
where Weeks 2, 3 and 4 are assessed together rather than separately.

## Final project — 40%, Week 12

End-to-end AI on a dataset you have not seen before and that general-purpose AI
assistants have not been trained on, delivered with a live demonstration and
viva. Assessed mainly on understanding, reproducibility and the defence, which
is what makes producing the artefacts with a language model neither sufficient
nor low-risk. The full brief is in Week 12.

> Weightings are indicative and tuned to programme and QA requirements.
