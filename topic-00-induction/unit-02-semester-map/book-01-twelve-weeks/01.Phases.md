# The Three Phases

## Phase I — Foundations and Ethics (Weeks 1 to 5, 50%)

Half the module. Data and statistics; supervised learning and its applications;
ethics, bias and fairness; AI safety, governance and explainability;
unsupervised learning.

Fairness sits at Week 3 deliberately, on the classical models, where the
metrics are simple enough that a fairness definition can be computed by hand
and argued about. Safety, governance and explainability follow at Week 4 on the
same models. Both return in the final project as a required audit rather than
an optional reflection.

## Phase II — Neural Networks and the Mathematics (Weeks 7 and 8, 20%)

Week 7 is dense by design: networks are built and trained in a single week, so
the lab does the from-scratch build while the lecture also covers training
well. If the cohort needs room, the lab can run across the Week 7 and Week 8
sessions.

Week 8 then consolidates the mathematics used in Weeks 5 and 7 — SVD,
information theory, the manifold hypothesis — and sets up transfer learning.

## Phase III — Modern and Science-Inspired Learning (Weeks 9 to 11, 30%)

Transfer and augmentation, transformers and attention, then diffusion, neural
ODEs and symmetry. Self-supervised pretraining is folded into Week 9 as the
objective behind foundation models, rather than taught as a separate week.
