## Exercise 2.2: Read the fit, don't just score it

**Task:** Plot the residuals (actual minus predicted) against the predictions:

```python
import matplotlib.pyplot as plt
model = Pipeline([("prep", build_prep(num_r, categorical)),
                  ("reg", LinearRegression())]).fit(Xr_tr, yr_tr)
resid = yr_te - model.predict(Xr_te)
plt.scatter(model.predict(Xr_te), resid, alpha=0.3, s=8)
plt.axhline(0, color="red")
plt.xlabel("predicted LIMIT_BAL"); plt.ylabel("residual"); plt.show()
```

Look at the shape. Is the cloud roughly even around zero, or does it fan out (a funnel) as predictions grow? A funnel would mean the error grows with the prediction, and a plain linear model in the raw units may not be the right tool. Write one sentence on what you see.
