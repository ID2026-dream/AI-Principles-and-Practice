# Week 6 Quiz Practice

The Weeks 1 to 5 quiz summary as web pages: sixty points, five checklists and a
hundred practice questions.

## How to use this lab

Weeks 1 to 5 are examined in the **Week 6 quiz**, an in-class quiz on Weeks 1
to 5. Everything it can ask about them is in the **sixty points** in this lab,
one step per week, each in lecture order.

Take one week per sitting. Read its points (about 20 minutes). Do its twenty
questions (about 20 minutes). Mark them against the **Answers** step at the
end. Re-read only the points you got wrong.

The same material is in the **Weeks 1 to 5 Quiz Summary** card in this unit as
a printable PDF.
