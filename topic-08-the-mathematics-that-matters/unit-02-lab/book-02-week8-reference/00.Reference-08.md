# Week 8 Reference Answers

Go through this beside your own sheet, after you have written your own answers. For each question you get the
reference answer with the numbers from a reference run, why, and for predictions, the tempting answer and
what makes it tempting. Your numbers may differ in the third decimal place; the pattern is the result.

## The five predictions people get wrong most often

| Question | The tempting answer | What actually happens |
|---|---|---|
| **1a** | (1, 0) and (0, 1) keep their direction, because they are "the axes" | Only (1, 1) and (1, −1) do, stretched ×4 and ×2 |
| **3a** | You need about 100 pieces for a good photo | 20 pieces give an error of 4.9%, with 28% of the numbers |
| **4b** | 70/20/10 is badly wrong, so KL is near 1 bit | 0.133 bits; it is the 90/8/2 forecaster who pays 0.81 |
| **5a** | 23 features must cut the surprise a lot | Only from 0.528 to 0.472 nats: 0.08 bits per customer |
| **6a** | A 10% peel stays about 10% of the orange | In 100 dimensions it is 99.997% |
