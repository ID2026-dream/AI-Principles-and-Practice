# Lab (2h 05min)

Six exercises, in the same order as the lecture. In each you predict first, run, and explain in your own words; in Exercises 2 and 4 you write the maths yourself: PCA via SVD, and entropy, cross-entropy and KL. In Exercise 6 you use your own project data. This lab is not graded: submit your SUBMISSION.md, then compare with the reference answers.
