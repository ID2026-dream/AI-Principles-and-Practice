# Going Further (optional)

Not part of the lab. For people who finished and want more.

- **Rank.** Change the LoRA rank in `ls.adapt("lora", 5, rank=...)` to 1, 2 and 8. How do accuracy and the numbers trained change?
- **More labels.** Run `ls.three_ways(k=20)`. Does the gap between the three ways grow or shrink?
- **A real foundation model.** On Google Colab, load a pretrained image model (for example ResNet-18 from `torchvision`) and train only a new final layer on a small image set: the same linear probe, at real scale.
- **Read more.** Hu and others (2021), *LoRA: Low-Rank Adaptation of Large Language Models*; Patterson and others (2021), *Carbon Emissions and Large Neural Network Training*.
