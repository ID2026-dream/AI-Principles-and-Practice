# Exercise 1 · Reuse it as it is

**1a · prediction.** **About 70%** (71.3%).
*The tempting answer:* about 95%, because the digits themselves have not changed.

**1b · record and explain.** Clean digits it has never seen: **98.2%**; the same digits from the noisy scanner:
**71.3%**. The backbone learned from clean scans only. Grainy noise and a bright blot are patterns it has never met,
so they push its numbers in directions its training never covered. The *job* is the same; the *conditions* changed.
