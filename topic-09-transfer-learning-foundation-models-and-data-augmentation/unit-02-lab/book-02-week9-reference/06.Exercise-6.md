# Exercise 6 · Your capstone

**6a.** Depends on your elective. E4 starts from pretrained image and text models (probe, then LoRA or fine-tune). E3
starts from a pretrained language model (prompt or probe, then LoRA). E1 and E2 usually train from scratch on tabular
data, with a probe on learned features as a comparison. E5 and E6 build in the physics instead of reusing a model.

**6b.** Examples. Images: a small shift or brightness change (true); flipping a digit or a house number (wrong). Text: a
typo or a synonym (usually true); changing a negation (wrong). Tabular health data: usually no safe augmentation, and
synthetic patients need great care.

**L1.** A linear probe trains only a new final layer on a frozen model. LoRA trains two thin strips added to frozen
weight tables. Augmentation teaches a model what does not change the answer.

**L2.** Your own answer. The most common: 2a, 4b or 1a.
