# Going Further (optional)

Not part of the lab. For people who finished and want more.

- **A fairer probe baseline.** The booklet (Section 16.4) suggests a second baseline: train the same probe on the same
  features with the labels **shuffled**. If your layer-6 probe does not clearly beat that, you learned about your probe,
  not about the model.
- **Every layer, every head.** Probe all seven hidden states (0 to 6), not just three, and plot accuracy against layer.
  Then compute all 72 heads' maps for one headline and find the heads that put most of their weight on `[CLS]` or
  `[SEP]` ("attention sinks", booklet Section 4).
- **Prompt brittleness, measured.** Rewrite the Section 2 prompt three ways that mean the same thing (for example
  `Topic:` instead of `Category:`) and measure how far the 4-shot accuracy moves.
- **Induction heads.** Feed GPT-2 a random block of tokens repeated twice and look for the shifted diagonal in the
  second half (booklet Section 9). Use `AutoModelForCausalLM.from_pretrained("gpt2", output_attentions=True,
  attn_implementation="eager")`.
