## Key takeaways

1. **Attention is content-based retrieval.** Query, key and value turn "which tokens matter here" into a dot product
   and a softmax; every row's budget sums to one.
2. **A strong simple baseline is the honest opponent.** TF-IDF plus a linear model gets about 0.924 on AG News in
   seconds, and a frozen, un-fine-tuned transformer often loses to it. Weak inductive bias only pays off with data,
   compute and fine-tuning behind it.
3. **In-context learning is real, and it scales.** Your frozen GPT-2 learned from its prompt with no weight change, but
   only modestly, which is just what a small model should do.
4. **Reading a model's inside is a discipline.** A probe shows a property is linearly decodable, no more; a heatmap
   shows what a head read, not why the model decided. Claiming exactly that much is LO4.
