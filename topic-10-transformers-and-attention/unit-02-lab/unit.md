# Lab (1 h)

Inside the transformer. You take two pretrained transformers, freeze them, and open them up: read and stress-test one attention head, watch in-context learning happen with the weights fixed, probe the hidden layers against an honest bag-of-words baseline, and measure the quadratic cost of attention. About 45 minutes of work and 15 of questions. **Download the models before the lab.** This lab is graded.
