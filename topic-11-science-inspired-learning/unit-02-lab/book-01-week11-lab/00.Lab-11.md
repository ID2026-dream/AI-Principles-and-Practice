# Week 11 Lab: Generating with Physics

> **You do not need the booklet for this lab.** Every question says which printout to read, and every exercise page starts with the idea **in plain words**, with a worked example that matches its check.

2,000 points on a spiral. You will dissolve them into noise, teach a small network to undo the noise, and generate
brand-new points from pure noise. You will treat layers as small steps of motion and learn a rule of motion. Then, as in
electives E5 and E6, you will teach a network with **zero data**, using only the equation the system obeys, and find an
unknown number with a simulator. Along the way you build in a symmetry, and break each idea on purpose.

Eight exercises, following the lecture, and every exercise works the same way:

| Step | What you do | Where it goes |
|---|---|---|
| **Predict** | Commit to an answer *before* running the cell | `SUBMISSION.md` |
| **Run** | One cell, sometimes two | the notebook |
| **Look at** | The printout or table the page names | the notebook |
| **Explain** | Two or three sentences in your own words | `SUBMISSION.md` |
| **Build** | One line of code, checked by a `check_my_...` cell | the notebook, then `SUBMISSION.md` |
| **Change one thing** *(Exercises 5, 6 and 7)* | Predict, change one thing, run, say what happened | both |

**A wrong prediction is not a mistake.** Write it before you run, and do not change it afterwards.
