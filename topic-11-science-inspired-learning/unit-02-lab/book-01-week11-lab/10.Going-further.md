# Going Further (optional)

Not part of the lab. For people who finished and want more.

- **Fewer steps.** Change the denoiser's training to 1,000 steps with `ls.Denoiser().train(1000)` and generate again. How do the samples change?
- **Memorisation.** In `inspect_samples`, 6% of points sit almost on top of a training point. What would you expect with only 50 training points?
- **A bigger step.** In `ls.langevin(my_langevin_step, step=0.2)`, what happens to the samples, and why?
- **Zero data, harder.** In `ls.zero_data(my_leftover, c_told=0.8)` the network is told the wrong friction. Compare its score and its real error with the right one.
- **Read more.** Raissi, Perdikaris and Karniadakis (2019), *Physics-informed neural networks*; Ho, Jain and Abbeel (2020), *Denoising Diffusion Probabilistic Models*; Chen and others (2018), *Neural Ordinary Differential Equations*; Bronstein and others (2021), *Geometric Deep Learning*.
