# Exercise 4 · Roll downhill, with a little shaking

**4a · build.** `return x - step * slope + np.sqrt(2 * step) * z`

**4b · prediction.** **About half in each** (50.6% left, 49.4% right).
*The tempting answer:* uneven, because points might get stuck in whichever valley is nearer to where they started. The shaking
lets them hop over the hill now and then, so over 500 steps both valleys fill evenly.

**4c · record and explain.** Average distance from 0: 0.859, against 0.868 exactly. Without shaking, every point rolls to the
bottom of its nearest valley and stops at exactly −1 or +1: no spread at all, so not samples of the energy's distribution.
With far too much shaking, points fly about everywhere and the valleys barely matter.
